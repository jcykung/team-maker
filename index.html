        Team Maker

        A quick and flexible tool for generating and managing teams, seating arrangements, or any kind of group assignment — works great on desktop and phone.

        1. Add Your Members

        Go to the Roster tab. Paste a list of names (one per line) or upload a CSV file. The tool automatically strips quotes, converts "Last, First" to "First Last", and skips duplicates.

        The Random Picker lets you call on someone at random from your full list, with an option to remove picked members from the pool so nobody gets picked twice.

        2. Generate & Edit Teams

        Go to the Teams tab. Choose Members per Team or Number of Teams, set the amount, then click Generate Teams.

        Use Constraints to lock pairs together or force them apart — the same constraints also apply in the Seating tab. The algorithm tries up to 500 attempts to satisfy every constraint. Adding a "Together" constraint when the pair is already set as "Apart" (or vice versa) will be blocked automatically.

        💻 On Desktop

        Drag member chips between team cards to reassign them. Drag a team card header to reposition it on the canvas. Use the Text Box button (under Projector Mode) to add labels, headings, or notes to the canvas — click any text box to select it and use the format toolbar to change font, size, bold, italic, or underline. Press Delete or Backspace while a team or text box is selected to remove it.

        📱 On Phone

        Teams appear as scrollable cards. Tap any member name to open a sheet to move them or remove them. Tap the fullscreen icon (⤢) to enter Projector Mode — tap Exit when done.

        3. Create Seating Arrangements

        Go to the Seating tab to design a room layout and assign students to seats.

        🎹 Keyboard Shortcuts

        V Select / Move / Resize / Rotate
        S Square seat
        C Circle seat
        U Unoccupied square
        O Unoccupied circle
        T Text Box (desktop)
        D Delete tool
        Del / ⌫ Delete selected seat / textbox
        Ctrl+Z Undo
        Ctrl+Y Redo

        Drawing: Choose Square or Circle and click to place a seat, or click-and-drag to paint a row. Unoccupied □/○ places a shape with a custom label (e.g. "Teacher's Desk") that won't be assigned a student. Use Text Box to add free-floating labels on desktop.

        Arranging: Use the Select tool (V) to click a seat and show resize and rotate handles. Right-click any seat for options including Split into seats, which turns one large table into labeled sections — rectangular tables spread names side by side, circular tables arrange them around the perimeter. After splitting, drag any name chip freely within the table.

        Assigning: Press Load Roster to populate the staging area, then Assign Seats to auto-assign everyone. Constraints apply: Together means adjacent seats (touching), Apart means opposite sides of the room. After assigning, drag any name chip between seats or to the staging area to adjust. Click × on any chip to return that student to the staging area.

        📐 Tips

        Snap On keeps seats aligned to the grid. Ctrl+scroll or trackpad pinch to zoom — the grid scales with zoom. Press Delete or Backspace while a seat is selected to remove it.

        4. Export

        Go to the Export tab for a print-ready preview. Toggle between Teams and Seating views. Download as PDF or JPG.

        5. Files & Auto-Save

        Everything auto-saves to your browser as you work — no save button needed. Click Files to manage multiple files (one per class, period, or session).

        Use Backup All Files to download a .json of everything. Restore it anytime with Restore from Backup.

        Tip: export a backup regularly — browser storage can be cleared when disk space is low.
