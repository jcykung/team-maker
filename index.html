      Team Maker

      A quick and flexible tool for generating and managing teams, seating arrangements, or any kind of group assignment — works great on desktop and phone.

      1. Add Your Members

      Go to the Roster tab. Paste a list of names (one per line) or upload a CSV file. The tool automatically strips quotes, converts "Last, First" format to "First Last", and skips duplicate names.

      The Random Picker on the same tab lets you call on someone at random from your full list, with an option to remove picked members from the pool so nobody gets picked twice.

      2. Generate & Edit Teams

      Go to the Teams tab. Choose whether to set the number of members per team or the number of teams, set the amount, then tap Generate Teams.

      Use Constraints to lock pairs together or force them apart before generating — the algorithm will try to respect every constraint across up to 500 attempts.

      💻 On Desktop

      Drag individual member chips between team cards to reassign them. Drag a team card's header to reposition it on the canvas — useful for mapping out a physical room layout. Use the zoom controls or pinch-to-zoom on a trackpad to fit everything on screen. Click Projector Mode to go fullscreen.

      📱 On Phone

      Teams are shown as scrollable cards. Tap any member's name to open a panel where you can move them to another team or remove them. Tap the fullscreen button (⤢) to enter Projector Mode — all teams fill the screen with no scrolling, portrait or landscape. You can still tap members to reassign them while in Projector Mode. Tap Exit when done.

      3. Export

      Go to the Export tab to see a clean print-ready preview of your teams. Download it as a PDF or JPG to share or print.

      The export automatically converts the dark canvas into a clean, light-background layout so it prints clearly.

      4. Files & Saving

      Your work is auto-saved in your browser. Click the Files button in the header to manage multiple saved files — handy if you run several groups or sessions and want to keep them separate.

      Use Backup All Files inside the Files panel to download a .json file containing everything. If you ever clear your browser storage or switch devices, you can restore everything from that backup with Restore from Backup.

      Tip: export a backup regularly — browser storage can be cleared by the browser when disk space is low.
